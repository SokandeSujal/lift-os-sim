How do the two algorithms work?

FCFS — first come, first served

Requests stay in arrival order. It is predictable and fair, but the lift may repeatedly cross the building because it will not skip an older stop.

Nearest-stop-first — SRTF idea

Every tick, the lift chooses its closest useful stop. It usually reduces waiting, but a distant person can wait longer if closer calls keep arriving.
